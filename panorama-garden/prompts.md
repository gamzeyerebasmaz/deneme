# Panorama Hotel Garden: Image-Editing Prompts

Upload the original photo with each prompt. Ground-level images (1, 2) use the vertical 3:4 photo. Bird's-eye images (3, 4) use the horizontal 4:3 photo.

**Order of use:** Generate Image 1 first. Upload it as a second reference with every later prompt and add: *"Match the furniture, parasols, plants and lights exactly to the second reference image."* Then generate 2, then 3, then 4. Generate 3 before 4 and also supply it as a reference for 4.

---

## Image 1: Ground-level, Model A (three fountain jets)

```
Edit this photo. Keep the exact camera position, vertical 3:4 framing, perspective and proportions. Preserve every mature cypress and pine tree, the overhanging branches at the top, the white balustrade fence across the back, the low building on the left, the white building and dark pergola on the right, and the sky. Do not enlarge the garden or move any structure.

Remove: the man, the cat, the rolled dark material by the right-hand wall, all hoses, cables and debris. Reduce the lens glare and haze in the top-right corner.

Replace the green artificial turf with lush, dense, real natural grass that has slight natural color variation. No paths, stepping stones, gravel or paving.

Water feature: one narrow, shallow, perfectly straight ornamental water channel, about 50 cm wide, running along the exact center of the lawn from the base of the white balustrade straight toward the camera and ending in the lower-center foreground. Irregular natural beige-gray stone borders, a bottom of rounded river pebbles, crystal-clear water. It is not a pool. Exactly three small, low fountain jets, evenly spaced along the channel, each about 30 cm high, with soft circular ripples. No waterfall.

Furniture: exactly 4 round natural-wood tables, each with 4 woven rattan armchairs with thick cream cushions. Two tables are left of the channel and two are right of it. The two foreground tables sit in the lower-left and lower-right thirds, close to the camera. The two rear tables are mid-ground, near the balustrade. Each table has one large cream parasol, a small vase of flowers and a warm table lamp.

Planting: pink and white hydrangeas, lavender, small flowers and ornamental grasses along both channel edges, with moderate planting under the trees. A Mediterranean boutique-hotel feel, not overcrowded.

Lighting: soft afternoon daylight with accurate tree shadows. Small, subtle warm-white landscape lights along the channel, with LEDs hidden between the rocks.

Real DSLR photograph, 35mm lens, f/5.6, natural color, realistic reflections on the water, slight natural imperfections. Not CGI.
```

**Negative:** people, animals, artificial turf, pool, waterfall, curved or diagonal channel, paths, stepping stones, gravel, paving, extra tables or parasols, plastic furniture, visible pumps or pipes, lens flare, CGI look, oversaturation, warped trees or buildings.
